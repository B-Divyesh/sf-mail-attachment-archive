`Try it with sample data`, `Download for Linux`, `Import the export`, `Resolve
and hash`, `Keep the exceptions`, `Buy Archive Plus`, `Have a license? Restore
it`, and `Get Mail Attachment Archive` are concise actions or labels rather
than prose sentences. The numeric sample line is `3 of 4 sample references
resolved`; its exact state is covered by `@claim:sample-evidence`.
