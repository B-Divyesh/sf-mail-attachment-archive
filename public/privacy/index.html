Privacy, in plain language

Your mailbox, messages, attachments, hashes, and reports stay on your computer. Mail Attachment Archive has no user account, telemetry, advertising, or analytics SDK.

Local archive data

The desktop app reads files you choose and writes to a folder you choose. It does not upload mail data. Encryption derives a key on-device, and the passphrase is not stored. Deleting the archive folder deletes that local archive.

License verification

If you buy or restore Archive Plus, the app or website sends only your license token to Sociobot’s billing API at most once per day. The result is cached locally. Sociobot/Dodo processes purchase and refund information as merchant of record. Mail data is never part of this request.

Website and demo

The site loads no third-party scripts, fonts, pixels, or analytics. The demo writes only its separate demo: key. The host may retain standard security logs such as IP address and request time.

Your choices

You can use the archive engine without a license. Clear site storage to remove a web license. Delete an archive folder to remove its desktop data.
