Terms of use

Mail Attachment Archive is a local utility for creating and verifying attachment archives from mail exports you control.

Your responsibility

You must have the right to process the mail you import. Keep an independent copy of the source MBOX until you have inspected the report and restored a sample of important files. Malware-bearing attachments are stored inert, but remain potentially harmful if restored and opened.

No silent promises

The app reports parsing and checksum failures; it cannot recover bytes that are absent from the source export. “Resolved” means the decoded bytes were stored locally and matched their recorded SHA-256 checksum, not that the file content is safe or semantically complete.
