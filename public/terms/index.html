Terms of use

Mail Attachment Archive creates and checks local attachment archives from mail exports you control.

Your responsibility

You must have the right to process imported mail. Keep an independent source copy until you inspect the report and restore important samples. Stored attachments remain potentially harmful if you restore and open them.

No silent promises

The app reports parsing and checksum failures. It cannot recover bytes absent from the source export. “Resolved” means the decoded bytes matched their SHA-256 checksum. It does not mean the content is safe or complete.

Archive Plus purchase

Archive Plus costs US $29 as a one-time purchase. It adds saved workspace conveniences. Core importing, deduplication, encryption, restoration, and reports are free. Sociobot/Dodo handles payment and refunds as merchant of record. A refund revokes the license.
